What this is

Trial & Code is a YouTube channel for clinical and statistical programmers. Each video takes one paper from conferences such as PharmaSUG and PHUSE, runs its code for real, and explains the idea in about six minutes. Videos are available in English, हिंदी, Español and 中文 (switch the audio track in the player settings).

Watch on YouTube: @trialandcode

Trial & Code publisher

"Trial & Code publisher" is a small desktop tool used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos, captions, thumbnails and translated titles through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users. See the privacy policy.
